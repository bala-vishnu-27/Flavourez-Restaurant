Frequently Asked Questions

🚀 How fast is your delivery service? +
Our dedicated express delivery fleet ensures your hot food reaches your doorstep within 30 minutes of placing your order. You can track your order live on our Track Order page!
🌿 Are your Veg and Non-Veg kitchens separated? +
Yes! We strictly maintain separate cooking stations, utensils, and oil friers for 100% Pure Vegetarian items to adhere to the highest hygiene and dietary standards.
🔥 Can I customize my dish's spice level? +
Absolutely! During checkout on our Order page, you can add custom cooking notes (e.g. Extra Spicy, Medium, Low Oil, No Onion/Garlic).
🎉 Do you accept party or catering orders? +
Yes, we cater for birthday parties, corporate lunches, and family gatherings with special bulk discounts. Contact us directly via our Contact page.
